About this practice

100 original question families · Functional Skills Maths Level 1. The navy-and-teal layout, fraction boxes, graph controls and paper-only working are retained.

Exam-style preparation

Timed rounds have Section A: 25 minutes and 14 marks, then Section B: 90 minutes and 42 marks. This original blueprint allocates 14 practice marks to underpinning skills and 42 to applied problems. These are design classifications, not Pearson-validated item judgements.

Mixed papers cover number, measures/shape/space and handling data. The bank includes worded formulae, percentages in multiples of five, simple interest, ratios, metric measures, area, cuboid volume, nets, mean/range, tables, graphs and simple probability. Questions, values and order vary; one round does not test every content statement.

In timed rounds, the graph task uses three practice marks: one for the full title/axis/scale setup and up to two for plotting. In learning mode, seven smaller checks provide more detailed feedback. These are local practice criteria, not a copied examination mark scheme.

All working stays on paper

Only answers, graph responses and optional tutor review scores are saved. There are no student working-out textboxes, scratchpads or handwriting canvases. Keep your written methods for your tutor. Answer checks alone do not award an official grade or predict a pass.

Graph dropdowns and digital plotting are learning supports, not replicas of paper graph drawing. Drawing and symmetry work should also be practised with a ruler, protractor and paper. Use official papers alongside this app.

CENTURY nugget codes

Codes are taken from the supplied FE-Mathematics-Course-Content-Guide.pdf, file pages 1–5, printed pages 41–45, for FE Mathematics Functional Skills Level 1. Each heat-map row has one code only. The paper-review column has been removed from the heat map, image, CSV and printed table.

The code targets one primary skill. Multi-skill questions can need more than one type of practice, so one code is not a complete diagnosis. When the guide does not name the exact task, the closest relevant broader listed nugget is used rather than inventing a code. These question-to-nugget links are suggested teaching mappings, not official Pearson or CENTURY endorsements.

Search for the code in your CENTURY account. This app does not log into CENTURY, assign work there or transmit results. Access to particular nuggets depends on your course account.
